I am a Ph.D. student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at University of California, San Diego and San Diego State University (joined Fall 2026), co-advised by [Prof. Tajana Rosing](https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~trosing/) and [Prof. Flavio Ponzina](https://flavio-ponzina.github.io/flavio-ponzina/index.html). Previously, I graduated with my M.S. (2026) and B.S. (2025) in Computer Science at University of California, San Diego. My primary research interests are energy-efficient space systems, particulary focusing on HW-SW co-design optimizations, thermal integration challenges, and integrated photonics.
