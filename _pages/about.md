Welcome, I am a first-year graduate student at University of California, San Diego pursuing a M.S. in Computer Science. I am a member of Prof. Tajana Simunic's System-Energy Efficient Lab [SeeLab](http://varys.ucsd.edu/).

I did my B.S. in Computer Science at UC San Diego, where I worked as a Research Assitant to Prof. Chi-Hua Chen in the Radiology Department.

### Research Interest
My primary research interests are Machine Learning on Internet-of-Things (IoT) devices and Robotics for real-world applications. My work has involved deep generative modeling, molecular dynamics, and few-shot learning.
